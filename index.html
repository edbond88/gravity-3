How it works

Three stars pull on each other with Newtonian gravity. There is no general formula for their motion — tiny differences grow until the system becomes chaotic, and usually one star is flung away. Then a new system starts.

  • Start — a random chaotic start, or a known solution: Lagrange's triangle, Euler's line, the figure-eight and other periodic orbits, or a stable binary with a distant star. Most solutions are unstable and fall apart after a few periods.
  • Masses — set each star's mass in solar masses. Changing one restarts the simulation. Orbits marked “equal masses” switch back to chaos.
  • Drag stars — pause, drag any star to a new place and press play.
